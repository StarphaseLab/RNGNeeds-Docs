# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I install RNGNeeds?

RNGNeeds is installed as a Package through Unity's Asset Store. Please visit our 
<a href={GettingStartedPage.Link}>{GettingStartedPage.Title}</a> 
section and follow the installation guide.

## Does RNGNeeds work with my version of Unity?

RNGNeeds is developed and tested on Unity 2020 LTS, but it supports all LTS versions. It's also tested up to the 2023 Alpha version.

## Can I use RNGNeeds with my custom objects?

Yes! RNGNeeds is designed to work with any value or reference type, be it Unity's or your own.

## I'm having performance issues in the Unity editor. What can I do?

If you're experiencing editor slowdowns, you can adjust the Inspector Refresh Mode to 'Optimized'. This setting repaints the inspector only during mouse drag, which can improve performance. However, other actions, such as changing settings, might feel less responsive.

## How can I influence the odds of a particular outcome?

RNGNeeds introduces the concept of Probability Influence Providers. With them, you can design rules to dynamically modify probabilities based on external factors.

## Can I prevent the same item from being selected consecutively?

Yes, RNGNeeds offers multiple repeat prevention techniques to avoid consecutive item selections.

## What kind of support is available if I run into issues?

Your satisfaction is our top priority. If you're having difficulties, please visit our 
<a href={SupportPage.Link}>{SupportPage.Title}</a>
to find receive guidance and options to get in touch.

## How can I suggest improvements or new features for RNGNeeds?

We welcome your feedback and suggestions! Feel free to share them with us via email or on our Discord server. We're always looking for ways to make RNGNeeds even better!
