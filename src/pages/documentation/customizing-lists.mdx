# Customizing Lists

While designing a game, we spend most of our times looking at Unity Inspectors. That is why the RNGNeeds' custom drawer for Probability List was designed to feel inviting with a wide 
variety of options to customize the appearance. ...

---

## Appearance & Colors

The most prominent feature of the drawer is the Stripe - set of colored blocks displaying probabilities in an intuitive way. 
In addition, the blocks display info about items which can be toggled by three buttons in the left section of the drawer.

(image of the buttons)

- **#** - show/hide index of item
- **i** - show/hide name or value of item
- **%** - show/hide probability percentage

Note that the info is hidden if for narrow blocks on the stripe. 
In this case, a mouse-over highlight still provides visual connection between items in list and their representation in the stripe. 
Hovering the mouse over block or item highlights them both. 

By default, individual colors are dimmed by their probability, with higher probable items being brighter. 


THEME Button - color palettes, 40 of them, organized into various categories. 
DimeTone - palettes with ten distinct colors for easy recognizing of high number of items
Gradients - good for incrementing or decrementing values. 
Duality - good for bi-values (true/false) or quad (north, east, south, west, or other)
Item Rarity - popular rarity color setups from known games
+ many other thematic palettes
Last option in dropdown - Monochrome - default color can be set in preferences window, but a custom monochrome color can be set for each drawer individually. Monochrome is great with color dimming, easily tell small differences.
stripe height - compact, short, normal, tall
